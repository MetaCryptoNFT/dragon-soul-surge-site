About the studio

Dragon Soul Surge is a one-person studio in Statesville, North Carolina. Everything here — the character, the scenes, the films, the worlds — is made in-house, one frame at a time.

Aethel is the through-line: a white-haired, ram-horned figure moving through golden boardwalks, off-world interiors, and rain-soaked streets. The work is about getting the light, the mood, and the moment right.
